# Attribution

research-paper-pilot is released under the MIT licence (see `LICENSE`). Every sentence in it was written fresh. No text was copied from the sources below; each contributed an idea, which was re-expressed in new words and generally merged with ideas from others. Licences are recorded as found when the sources were surveyed in October 2026. Check the upstream repositories for their current terms.
